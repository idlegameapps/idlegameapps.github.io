Little Paws Café support

Questions, bugs or ideas? Email idlegameapps@gmail.com and we'll get back to you.

Where is my progress saved?

On your phone. Deleting the game deletes your café, cats and coins.

Does the game need internet?

No. It works fully offline, and your café keeps earning for up to 8 hours while the game is closed.

Are there ads or in-app purchases?

No. You buy the game once and that's it.

How do I start over?

Tap the settings button on the Café tab, then Reset progress. This can't be undone.

How do I change the language or dark mode?

In the same settings: Language, and Appearance (System, Light or Dark).
