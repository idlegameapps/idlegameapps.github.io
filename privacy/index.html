Privacy policy

Little Paws Café doesn't collect any data about you.

Saved on your phone

Your café, cats and settings are stored only on your device. Deleting the game deletes them.

Nothing leaves your phone

The game works offline and never sends data anywhere. There are no accounts.

No ads or tracking

No ads, analytics or third-party services. Nothing is shared or sold.

Safe for all ages

Nothing is collected from anyone, children included.

If this policy changes, the new version will appear here and in the game.
